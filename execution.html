LIVE COMPRESSION

From correct reading to disciplined action.

Do not let an attractive pattern override regime, control, location, evidence, or the trader’s equation.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Operating rules

  • Regime sets the default expectation; price action must earn an exception.
  • Damage is not automatically a flip.
  • A breakout is a claim; follow-through determines acceptance.
  • Structure sets the stop; risk budget sets size.
  • Nearest reasonable destination first.
  • Every add must independently earn its existence.
  • If the thesis or equation is unclear, wait or do nothing.
PRE-ENTRY SCAN

The 11 questions

  1. What state is the market in?
  2. Who has control: AIL, AIS, or uncertain?
  3. What has each side actually accomplished?
  4. What phase is the move in?
  5. Where is price relative to structure and magnets?
  6. What is the current attempt?
  7. Did it receive follow-through?
  8. Whose expectation failed and who may be trapped?
  9. What is the nearest reasonable destination?
  10. Does probability × reward justify failure × risk?
  11. Enter, manage, exit, or wait?
STRUCTURE BEFORE COMFORT

Risk, stops, and management

Define structural invalidation before entry. If the correct stop is wider than usual, reduce size rather than placing the stop inside normal market noise. Do not move to breakeven merely because price moved slightly in your favor. If you would not enter the same trade now while flat, exit rather than anchoring to the entry price.

Management must remain consistent with the premise. A swing justified by asymmetric reward cannot be converted into a tiny scalp simply because open profit feels fragile. Reassess when control, structure, acceptance, or the equation materially changes.
