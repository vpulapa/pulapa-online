About

Engineering taste with a researcher's patience.

Pulapa is where software engineering, AI, and market-structure research meet. The goal is not to make flashy demos. It is to build tools that explain their assumptions, preserve evidence, and make better decisions easier for humans.

The work here is especially interested in AI-assisted workflows, trading research infrastructure, knowledge systems, and the craft of turning complex domains into usable interfaces.

AI

Tools that make reasoning visible.

Practical AI work belongs close to the evidence. Pulapa focuses on systems that can retrieve context, expose uncertainty, and keep a human in the loop.
