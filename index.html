REGIME MAP

Default expectation before exception

The environment defines what evidence deserves the most weight.

Strong trend

Default: continuation

One side repeatedly accomplishes. Countertrend bars are weak or quickly rejected. Seek participation, not a top or bottom.

Healthy / maturing channel

Default: continuation, selective

Directional advantage persists, but two-sidedness grows. Demand favorable structure and a credible resumption.

Broad trading range

Default: failed BO / oscillation

Both sides undo one another. Trade only meaningful edges and tested failures; avoid the middle.

Tight range / BO mode

Default: wait

Direction is unresolved. Require displacement, follow-through, and the ability to hold outside.

Transition / reversal environment

Default: old control damaged

New control has not yet been earned. The outcome may be resumption, range, or genuine reversal.

BEHAVIORAL ENGINES

Four sequences underneath the labels

01 / ACCEPTANCE

Claim → follow-through → acceptance or rejection

A breakout makes a claim. What follows determines whether the market believes it.

02 / CONTROL

Attempt → response → accomplishment

Effort matters through what it changes: structure, value, control, or trapped participation.

03 / FAILURE

Expectation → failure → trap → forced exit → fuel

A useful failure requires a clear expectation and participants who must respond.

04 / REVERSAL

Damage → test → failure → flip

Exit threshold is lower than reversal threshold. Opposite control must be earned.
